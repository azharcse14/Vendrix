## Open question (design e nai, user ke jiggesh; 16.1 ar STATUS e o)

1. **Shop `staff` er stock entry te variant khoja** (16.1 v1.37; 11.2 e staff "stock entry ar wastage" pare, kintu `GET /admin/products` owner/admin/vendor_owner).
   Options: (a) Notun shoru endpoint `GET /api/admin/inventory/variants?q=&low=&page=` (owner, admin, staff, vendor_owner, vendor_staff; vendor role e shudhu nijer vendor): `{variant_id, product_id, product_name, variant_label, sku, stock, low_stock_at, batch_remaining, next_expiry}`, naam/SKU search; `/inventory` screen er list-o eta; product er dam, kena dam, draft, SEO staff pay na. (b) `GET /admin/products`, `/products/{id}` read-only `staff` ar `vendor_staff` keo (write ager moto). (c) Shudhu SKU likhe (`GET /admin/variants?sku=`), list na.
   Recommendation: (a). Least privilege (b te `cost_price` ar unpublished product o dekhe), ar inventory screen er list ek query tei; (c) te staff ke SKU mukhosto rakhte hoy.
2. **Batch, stock ar adjust er niyom** (8.12; `batch_expiry` off hole ki).
   Options: (a) Stock barano shudhu `POST /batches` (purchase: `variant_id, quantity, cost_price?, batch_code?, harvest_date?, expiry_date?`; received = remaining, `variant.stock + qty`, movement `purchase` `batch_id` shoho, ek tx). `POST /inventory/adjust` shudhu komano (`change < 0`, `reason` `wastage` ba `adjust`, `note` 1–500 badhyotamulok): `batch_id` dile oi batch theke, na dile FIFO kram e (6.4 er query); `variant.stock` o kome, `stock` er beshi hole `409 out_of_stock` `details {available}` (open order er jonno rakha stock waste kora jay na); batch na thaka purono stock e shudhu `variant.stock`. Bhul gona thik korte (+) notun batch (`batch_code` jemon "count"). `batch_expiry` off hole-o batch row hoy (kena dam, recall), kintu pack e FIFO ar `order_item_batches` na (8.11 item 2) ar mil/expire job na. (b) `adjust` duidikei (+ hole batch chhara shudhu stock). (c) `batch_expiry` off e batch na, shudhu `adjust` ±.
   Recommendation: (a). Stock barar ekta-i path, tai 8.12 er "Mil" (`sum(batch remaining) = stock + open qty`) bhange na; (b) te + adjust batch chhara stock barale FIFO te batch kom pore pack atke.
3. **Expiry job ar alert M7 e** (8.12 table er protidin job, 8.15 item 2 er ghontay job).
   Options: (a) M7 e shudhu `inventory.expire` (protidin shop er timezone e 00:10, `batch_expiry` on shop e: expire batch er `quantity_remaining` 0, `variant.stock` theke `LEAST(qty, stock)` komano, movement `expired`, idempotent); low stock, expiry kache (`inventory.expiry_alert_days`, default 7), deadline kache shudhu dashboard ar `GET /inventory/alerts` er query; alert job (low stock, mil, deadline, `alert.batch_short`) ekhon `slog`, email/webhook M12 e. (b) Shob job M7 e (`slog` alert). (c) Kono job na, shudhu query.
   Recommendation: (a). Expire na hole expire maal `variant.stock` e theke checkout e bikri hoy (FIFO pack e atke, customer er order atke); baki gulo dekhar jinish, dashboard-i jothesto limited launch e.
