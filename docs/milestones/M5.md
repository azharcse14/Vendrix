## Open question (uttor: shob recommendation, design v1.28)

1. **Guest cart key ke banay, format ar TTL** (8.4, 10.3: "`__Host-cart=<random>`", 7.1 "`X-Cart-Key: <random>`"; ke banay, kemon, kotodin bola nai).
   Options: (a) Go banay: key chhara (ba ochena/bhul format er key) guest `POST /api/cart/items` e notun `rand.Text()` (128 bit, base32 26 okkhor), DB te `sha256`, response e `cart_key`; BFF body theke muche `__Host-cart` (`HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax; Path=/; Max-Age=2592000`) set kore (`_login` er moto); protiti cart write e BFF cookie abar set kore (sliding 30 din, DB er 30 din `updated_at` er sathe mile). Go shudhu `^[A-Z2-7]{26}$` key mane, baki key "nai" dhore. (b) BFF banay (`Random.secure` 32 byte base64url) ar cookie set kore, Go format check kore. (c) Browser JS banay.
   Recommendation: (a). Key er entropy ar format ek jaygay (session token er moto), app (Phase 2) eki path pay; client nijer pochhonder key diye cart banate pare na; GET kokhono row banay na (bot er GET e DB bhore na).
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
2. **Login ar key duitai thakle, ar bhul/expire token** (7.4 "login ba `X-Cart-Key`").
   Options: (a) Bearer thakle shudhu account cart, key ignore; Bearer ache kintu bhul/expire hole `401` (`RequireCustomer` er moto), guest e fall back na; notun `httpx.OptionalCustomer` (Bearer na thakle guest, thakle `RequireCustomer` er path). (b) Bearer bhul hole guest cart e fall back. (c) Duita cart mile dekhano.
   Recommendation: (a). Ekta-i niyom (Rule 16 er session check hubohu), expire session chupchap guest hoye bhul cart dekhay na; merge shudhu verify e.
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
3. **Merge er niyom** (8.4: "quantity jog, stock e cap").
   Options: (a) Qty jog, `min(jog, 50, stock)`; stock 0 hole item thake `min(jog, 50)` (out of stock dekhay, stock ferot ashle kena jay); bondho product bad; guest cart (row) muche; dui cart `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` (account cart na thakle toiri) verify er transaction e; website e verify `200` hole `__Host-cart` `Max-Age=0`. (b) Qty jog na, `max(duita)` (duibar add kora "bhul" double hoy na). (c) Guest cart account cart ke replace kore.
   Recommendation: (a). 8.4 er lekha (jog + cap) hubohu; stock 0 er item muche fellale customer jane na keno harailo.
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
4. **Stock komle cart er qty** (8.4: "beshi hole stock ar 50 er moddhe"; add er por stock komle ki, bola nai).
   Options: (a) `GET /api/cart` normalize kore: qty > stock (stock > 0) hole qty = `min(stock, 50)` (DB te o) + notice `quantity_reduced`; stock 0 hole item thake, `in_stock: false`, subtotal e na; checkout (M6) abar dekhe. (b) GET kichu bodlay na, item e `max_quantity` ar flag; customer nije komay. (c) Stock 0 hole-o bad.
   Recommendation: (a). Cart e ja dekhay ta-i kena jay, subtotal shotti; stock 0 er item rakhle "abar ashle kinbo" thake. Add/patch e (customer er nijer kaj) chupchap komano na, `409 out_of_stock` + `max_quantity`. Item e `max_quantity = min(stock, 50)` dey (exact stock 50 er niche dekha jay; catalog e shudhu `in_stock`, eta mene nite hobe).
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
5. **Bondho product kokhon bad** (8.4: "cart theke bad, response e notice").
   Options: (a) `GET /api/cart` e: product/variant `is_active = false` (ba vendor `active` na) er item `DELETE ... RETURNING` (ek query), response `notices: [{code: "item_removed", name}]` (naam bortoman bhasha e); POST/PATCH e bondho variant `404 not_found`. (b) GET e lukano, row rakha (chalu hole ferot ashe), `cart.cleanup` e muche. (c) Admin product bondho korle shob cart theke muche (M7 er code e).
   Recommendation: (a). Acceptance "cart theke bad" sorasori; notice ekbar-i ashe (porer GET e row nai); admin er code cart jane na. Variant `404` (`409 out_of_stock` na), karon public catalog e-o bondho variant nai.
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
6. **Dam bodlale customer ke janano** (8.4: cart e dam save hoy na).
   Options: (a) Na: cart shob shomoy bortoman dam dekhay (`compare_at_price` thakle kata dam); dam bodle gele checkout e `expected_total` (8.6 step 12) `409 price_changed` dhore (M6). (b) `cart_items.seen_price` column (`002` migration) ar notice `price_changed`. (c) Website client e ager dam mone rakhe.
   Recommendation: (a). 8.4 ichchha kore dam rakhe na; migration ar notun state lage na; asol jhuki (order er dam) M6 er `price_changed` e dhora.
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
7. **Cart er item limit ar abuse**.
   Options: (a) Ek cart e max 50 ta alada variant; 51 tomo add e `409 cart_full` (notun code, 7.1 + l10n); guest cart toiri public limit (IP te minute e 120) diye, alada limit na; body te `quantity` max 50 (50 er beshi `400 validation_failed` na, `409 out_of_stock` + `max_quantity` jate UI ek niyom e chole). (b) Limit nai. (c) Max 100 variant, ar IP te ghontay N ta notun guest cart.
   Recommendation: (a). Organic shop e 50 variant onek; limit chhara ek cart e hajar row `GET` ke dhire kore. Notun cart limit lage na: row choto, 30 din e muche, 120/minute IP limit.
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).
8. **Guest wishlist cookie `wl` kivabe, kokhon merge** (10.3: `wl` "product ID list", `Secure; SameSite=Lax`, Max-Age nai; 7.4 merge endpoint; 10.9 e merge route nai).
   Options: (a) `wl` = koma diye product ID (decimal), max 50, `Path=/; Secure; SameSite=Lax; Max-Age=1 bochor`, HttpOnly na: `@client WishlistButton` guest e cookie nijei lekhe (server e jay na), login e BFF `/_/wishlist/{id}` (Go). Merge: BFF er verify route `200` er por `wl` thakle notun token diye `POST /api/wishlist/merge`, tarpor `wl` `Max-Age=0`; merge fail hole login fail na (log). Go merge e shudhu oi shop er active product, ochena/bhul ID bad, max 50 ID, wishlist e mot 200. (b) `wl` HttpOnly, BFF route diye lekha (guest e o server), `_personal` e jog. (c) Guest wishlist na (login chhara button login e niye jay).
   Recommendation: (a). Product page public cache thake (guest er heart JS e), notun server state na; merge login er ek jaygay (cart er moto). Login kora user er heart er shuru obostha: SSR e `GET /api/wishlist` (oi page `__Host-sid` er jonno already `private, no-store`).
   Uttor: (a) (user, 15 November 2026).

   Options: (a) Product page e `@client AddToCart` (quantity stepper + button; JSON e BFF, uttor e badge/lekha; JS chhara `<form method=post action=/_/cart/items>` → `303 /cart`); `/cart` page e `@client` na: protiti item e JS chhara form (qty `PATCH`, bad `DELETE`, BFF er `apiMethod`; notun BFF path `POST /_/cart/items/{id}/delete`), `303` e page abar render, total server er.
