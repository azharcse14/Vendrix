# Bhasha, taka ar tarikh (shared)

Design: 9.8, 4.3, 2.3 · Milestone: M2 · Code: `packages/shared/lib/src/format`

## Ki kore

Website, admin ar app ekoi niyome taka dekhay: `৳১,২৫,০০০` (Bangla) ba `৳1,25,000` (English digit), lakh-style comma, ৳ samne, poisha thakle duita doshomik. Database e taka shob shomoy integer poisha.

## Kivabe kaj kore

1. `numberLocale(uiLocale, banglaDigits:)` (`format/money.dart`): UI bhasha `bn` ar shop er `locales.bangla_digits` on hole `bn`, na hole `en_IN` (v1.12).
2. `formatTaka(poisha, locale)`: poisha 100 diye bhag gele `NumberFormat('#,##,##0', locale)`, na hole `#,##,##0.00`; samne ৳, minus hole `-৳` (v1.12). `NumberFormat.currency` na (tar `bn` pattern e ৳ pichone, shob shomoy doshomik).
