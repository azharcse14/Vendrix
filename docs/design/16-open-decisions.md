### 16.2 Assumption

1. MVP te ekta shop, ekta house vendor, ekta warehouse.
2. Shob dam taka (BDT), bidesh e bikri nai.
3. Customer der beshirbhag mobile e, Facebook theke.
4. Staff 1–10 jon.
5. Ek order e ekta delivery thikana.
